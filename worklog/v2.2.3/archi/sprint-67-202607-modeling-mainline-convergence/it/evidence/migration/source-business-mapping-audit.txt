Sprint-67 F2-T03 canonical source-business mapping dependency audit
Verified: 2026-07-19 Asia/Shanghai
Result: PASS for canonical baseline removal; legacy compatibility remains intentionally present
Implementation commit: 72300c2ce

Source audit:
- WarehousePlanApplicationService.loadBaseline delegates to
  WarehousePlanContract.evaluateBaseline(categoryScope, sourceInventory, planningPolicy, onboardingMode).
- The canonical overload has no SourceBusinessMapping argument and computes readiness from category, source inventory, policy and onboarding mode only.
- WarehousePlanSourceInventoryApplicationServiceIT.businessFirstConceptualDesignCanProceedWithoutSourcesOrLegacyMappings
  asserts a BUSINESS_FIRST plan reaches the conceptual baseline with zero mapping rows.
- WarehousePlanSourcesTab registers catalog assets directly as source bindings and never requests a business object or business process.

Compatibility boundary intentionally retained:
- PUT /api/modeling/warehouse-plans/{id}/baseline/source-mappings still exists for legacy callers;
- SourceBusinessMapping and the old evaluateBaseline overload remain readable during the F5 controlled-retirement window;
- no table, column or compatibility API was dropped in F2-T03 Phase A.

Conclusion:
- source-business mapping is no longer a canonical baseline prerequisite;
- the legacy surface is compatibility-only and must be frozen/audited/removed under F5, not silently deleted here;
- candidate preview/confirm Phase B must call the objectless ModelSpec API and must not revive mapping as a gate.
